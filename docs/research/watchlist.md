## How an entry leaves this list

A primary source (Anthropic engineering/news/docs, or the vendor's own
documentation) that names the mechanism. Then it becomes a plan item with a
metric, or an explicit non-goal, and this row is deleted. Commentary, however
widely shared, does not move a row.
